Your Academic Pathway

Structured support across each stage of the academic journey, from early foundations to final applications.

Year 10

What students need:

As students begin more demanding academic study, many need stronger mathematical foundations, better study habits, and greater confidence approaching unfamiliar problems.

What Hills offers:

  • · Academic enrichment beyond standard school curricula
  • · Oxford-style problem-solving discussions
  • · Structured mentoring in mathematics and science
  • · Guidance on study habits, independent learning, and academic organisation
  • · Early preparation for advanced academic pathways and competitions
Year 11

What students need:

As academic difficulty increases, students often need support moving beyond memorisation towards deeper understanding and more independent problem solving. Many students also begin exploring future university pathways and competitive academic opportunities.

What Hills offers:

  • · Advanced support in reasoning in mathematics and science
  • · Competition-style problem solving and extension work
  • · Structured academic guidance beyond the school syllabus
  • · Early preparation for admissions tests and super-curricular exploration
Year 12

What students need:

At this stage, students aiming for Oxbridge, Imperial, LSE and other top universities often need to develop more independent academic thinking. Many students begin preparation for competitions, admissions tests, and university-level academic discussion.

What Hills offers:

  • · Specialist preparation for STEP, BMO, BPhO, UKCSQ, and other academic competitions
  • · Advanced academic mentoring through university-level reading and discussion
  • · Personalised tutorials focused on deeper understanding rather than memorisation
  • · Mentoring to help students explore subjects beyond the school syllabus
Year 13

What students need:

During the final stage of applications, students must learn to think clearly under pressure, communicate ideas effectively, and approach unfamiliar academic questions with confidence.

What Hills offers:

  • · Structured preparation for ESAT, TMUA, and other admissions tests
  • · Oxbridge-style mock interviews focused on academic discussion and reasoning
  • · Guidance on personal statements and academic reflection
  • · One-to-one mentoring tailored to each student's target course and university
  • · Interview preparation designed to develop confidence, clarity, and independent thinking

His work at Hills reflects a simple principle: students succeed not by knowing more, but by thinking more clearly.
